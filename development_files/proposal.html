1. Executive Summary

The Smart Hospital Parking Management System is designed to automate vehicle entry, hospital visitor validation, parking fee calculation, payment, and exit control. The solution combines ANPR (Automatic Number Plate Recognition), webhook-based integration, QR validation, hospital appointment integration, automated boom barriers, online payment, and configurable whitelist/blacklist rules.

🏥 Core Business Principle
The system does not attempt to determine whether a person is a genuine hospital visitor from ANPR alone. Instead, ANPR identifies the vehicle and the hospital system validates the related patient, appointment, visitor, staff, emergency, or other authorized status.

Primary Objectives

  • Provide a fast, contactless vehicle entry and exit experience.
  • Automatically identify vehicles using ANPR and create a parking session.
  • Allow patients and visitors to validate their visit through appointment QR, reception registration, or integrated hospital systems.
  • Provide a configurable validation/grace period, proposed at 30 minutes.
  • Automatically convert unvalidated sessions into chargeable parking sessions.
  • Calculate parking charges automatically and display the amount at exit.
  • Enable QR-based digital payment and automatically open the exit barrier after confirmed payment.
  • Support white-listed, black-listed, staff, emergency, and other special vehicle categories.
  • Provide real-time monitoring, audit trails, analytics, and revenue reports.

2. Scope of the Solution

2.1 In Scope

  • Entry ANPR camera integration through HTTP/HTTPS webhook.
  • Vehicle image and number plate event capture.
  • Entry lane authorization and boom barrier control.
  • Parking session creation and lifecycle management.
  • Appointment/visitor validation through QR and hospital reception.
  • WhatsApp/email delivery of appointment and parking QR codes.
  • 30-minute configurable visitor validation window.
  • Automatic charging after validation window expires.
  • Exit ANPR and parking session lookup.
  • Real-time tariff calculation.
  • Payment QR display and payment gateway integration.
  • Payment success webhook and automatic barrier release.
  • Whitelist and blacklist management.
  • Staff and emergency vehicle handling.
  • Real-time dashboard, reports, audit logs, and alerts.

2.2 Recommended Out-of-Scope / Integration Dependencies

  • Clinical decisions and patient eligibility decisions remain with the hospital's HIS/EMR/appointment system.
  • Payment acquiring, bank settlement, and payment gateway compliance are handled by the selected payment provider.
  • ANPR camera accuracy depends on camera positioning, lighting, plate format, weather, and vendor capabilities.
  • Boom barrier safety mechanisms such as induction loops and obstruction sensors remain under the barrier/traffic-control hardware layer.

3. High-Level System Architecture

The solution follows an event-driven architecture. ANPR cameras generate entry/exit events, the Parking Management Application applies business rules, and an Action Controller/relay interface operates the boom barrier.

HIS / Appointment↔ Secure API↔ Parking App
WhatsApp / Email QR→ Validation Service→ Parking Session
Payment Display QR→ Payment Gateway→ Success Webhook→ Exit Barrier

5. Hospital Visitor Validation Process

The visitor validation process determines whether the active parking session qualifies for free or discounted parking. The proposed default validation window is 30 minutes, configurable by the hospital.

5.1 Validation Method A — Appointment QR

  • Appointment is created in the hospital appointment system.
  • Parking entitlement/QR token is generated by the Parking System or hospital integration.
  • QR is delivered through WhatsApp/email/SMS as configured.
  • Visitor presents QR at the designated scanner or reception.
  • System validates token, appointment date/time, status, and applicable parking entitlement.
  • The QR token is associated with the active vehicle parking session.
  • Parking session status changes to VALIDATED / FREE or DISCOUNTED according to policy.

5.2 Validation Method B — Reception

  • Visitor approaches reception without a usable appointment QR.
  • Reception searches appointment/patient/visitor details.
  • Reception selects or enters the vehicle number.
  • System identifies the active parking session.
  • Reception confirms the visit.
  • Parking session is marked VALIDATED and the entitlement is recorded.

5.3 Validation Method C — Automatic Vehicle/Appointment Match

⭐ Best Future Experience
During appointment booking, the patient can optionally register a vehicle number. When ANPR detects that vehicle, the system can automatically match the vehicle to the appointment and validate the parking session without requiring the patient to scan a QR.

6. Non-Validated Vehicle & Parking Charging

If a vehicle enters the hospital parking area but the visit is not validated within the configured grace/validation window, the system automatically converts the session to a chargeable parking session.

6.1 Charging Example

EventExample
Entry10:15 AM
Validation window30 minutes
Charging starts10:45 AM
TariffExample: BHD 0.100 per 30 minutes
11:15 AMBHD 0.100
11:45 AMBHD 0.200
12:15 PMBHD 0.300

The tariff above is only an example. The hospital administrator can configure free periods, slabs, hourly/half-hourly rates, maximum daily caps, and discounts.

6.2 Exit Payment Flow

  • Exit ANPR detects the vehicle number and retrieves the active session.
  • System evaluates session state; for chargeable sessions, the tariff engine calculates the current amount.
  • Exit display displays amount and dynamic QR; customer scans to pay via mobile banking/cards.
  • Payment gateway webhook confirms payment; Action Controller automatically releases the boom barrier.
🔐 Payment Security Rule
The barrier must open only after the server has received and verified a payment success response from the payment provider. Do not treat a customer-facing screen alone as proof of payment.

7. Detailed Process Flow — Vehicle Exit

DecisionSystem Action
Vehicle is VALIDATED / FREEDisplay 'No Payment Required' and open barrier.
Vehicle is WHITELISTEDApply configured staff/authorized rule and open barrier.
Vehicle is EMERGENCY / PRIORITYApply emergency policy and open barrier.
Vehicle is CHARGING and amount = 0Open barrier if tariff rules allow zero charge.
Vehicle is CHARGING and amount > 0Display amount + payment QR and keep barrier closed until payment confirmation.
Vehicle is already PAIDOpen barrier and close session after exit confirmation.
Vehicle is BLACKLISTEDApply configured security rule; normally keep barrier closed and alert security.
Session not foundSend to exception/manual review workflow rather than guessing.

7.1 Exit Safety and Anti-Tailgating

  • Use vehicle presence/loop sensors in addition to ANPR.
  • Configure barrier auto-close timing and obstruction detection.
  • Use a second camera or lane sensor where practical for exit confirmation.
  • Prevent duplicate exit events from creating duplicate sessions.
  • Keep an operator override available for legitimate exceptions.
🛡 Anti-Tailgating Sensor Interlocking
The barrier logic counts loop activation and deactivation cycles. If a second vehicle attempts to follow without an authorized session, the barrier immediately lowers after the first vehicle clears, and an alert sounds.

8. Vehicle Access Control — White List, Black List & Priority

8.1 White List

  • Hospital staff vehicles
  • Doctors and management vehicles
  • Hospital-owned vehicles
  • Authorized vendor/service vehicles
  • Other permanently authorized vehicles

White-listed rules should be configurable by vehicle, category, gate, date/time, and validity period.

8.2 Black List

  • Restricted vehicles
  • Vehicles with repeated parking/security violations
  • Vehicles blocked by hospital security
  • Other vehicles specifically denied access
🔒 Security Principle
Show the driver a controlled message such as 'ACCESS DENIED — PLEASE CONTACT SECURITY'. Detailed blacklist reasons should normally be visible only to authorized security/administration users.

8.3 Priority / Emergency Vehicles

  • Ambulances
  • Emergency response vehicles
  • Fire/service vehicles where applicable
  • Other hospital-defined priority vehicles

Priority vehicles should have a separate policy rather than being treated as ordinary white-listed vehicles, because they may require immediate access even when normal parking rules are active.

12. Security, Reliability & Operational Controls

  • Use HTTPS for ANPR webhooks and application APIs wherever supported.
  • Authenticate camera/webhook sources using API keys, signed requests, IP allowlisting, or vendor-supported secure methods.
  • Do not place patient identifiers or sensitive medical information inside QR codes.
  • Use short-lived, single-use or controlled-use QR tokens.
  • Apply role-based access control for reception, security, parking administration and system administrators.
  • Maintain an audit trail for validation, blacklist changes, tariff changes, payment events, barrier commands and manual overrides.
  • Encrypt sensitive data in transit and at rest according to hospital security requirements.
  • Keep image retention configurable according to hospital policy and applicable privacy requirements.
  • Provide backup/recovery procedures for application and database services.
  • Provide local/manual fallback procedures for network or server outages.
  • Use barrier safety sensors and do not bypass the manufacturer's safety interlocks.
  • Design all webhook processing to be idempotent so duplicate camera/payment events do not create duplicate transactions.

12.1 Manual Override

  • Security/Admin can manually open a barrier when authorized.
  • Every manual override must require an authenticated user and reason.
  • Manual actions must be logged with timestamp, lane, vehicle and operator.
  • Repeated manual overrides should appear in an exception report.

⚡ 13.1 Webhook Processing Concept
Each external webhook should be stored as an event before business processing. The system should acknowledge the webhook quickly, then process it safely using an event queue or transactional service. This protects the lane from slow external integrations.

14. Exception & Edge-Case Handling

ScenarioRecommended Handling
ANPR cannot read plateCapture image, create MANUAL_REVIEW event and provide security/operator workflow.
Duplicate ANPR eventUse event ID/time/vehicle/lane rules to de-duplicate.
Appointment not foundKeep VALIDATION_PENDING and allow reception/manual validation until deadline.
QR expiredReject QR with clear message and route to reception if appropriate.
Vehicle changes during visitAuthorized staff can update the active session with audit trail.
Payment failsKeep barrier closed; allow retry or alternate payment method.
Network outageUse configured local fallback/manual process; synchronize events when connectivity returns.
Tailgating attemptSensor detects unauthorized follow; barrier auto-drops and triggers alert.
⚠️ 14.1 Important Rule — Never Guess
When the system cannot confidently determine the vehicle's status, it should move to MANUAL_REVIEW rather than automatically charging, waiving payment, or opening a restricted barrier.

15.1 Success Criteria

  • Fast and reliable entry/exit without unnecessary queue formation.
  • Accurate creation and closure of parking sessions.
  • Correct association of visitor validation with vehicle sessions.

16. Final Recommended End-to-End Process

The following is the recommended production workflow based on the proposed hospital use case:

1
VEHICLE ARRIVES → Entry ANPR detects plate and captures image.
2
WEBHOOK → ANPR event is securely pushed to Parking Application.
3
ACCESS CHECK → System evaluates blacklist/whitelist/priority rules.
4
ENTRY BARRIER → If allowed, Action Controller opens the boom barrier.
5
SESSION CREATED → Parking session starts as VALIDATION_PENDING.
6
VALIDATION → Patient/visitor validates by automatic appointment match, QR scanner, WhatsApp/email QR, or reception.
7
VALIDATED → Session becomes FREE/DISCOUNTED according to hospital policy.
8
NOT VALIDATED → After configurable 30-minute window, session becomes CHARGING.
9
EXIT ANPR → Vehicle number is identified and active session is retrieved.
10
EXIT DECISION → Validated/whitelist/priority = free exit; charging = payment required.
11
PAYMENT → Amount and QR are displayed; payment is verified by gateway webhook.
12
EXIT BARRIER → Verified free/paid authorization is sent to relay and barrier opens.
13
SESSION CLOSED → Exit time, amount, payment and barrier event are recorded.
14
REPORTING → Dashboard and reports update in real time.
